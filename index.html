How it works

Three simple layers that connect local craft knowledge with curious visitors.

1. About Us

About page represents our idea and the solution we are providing to the problem.

2. AR heritage walks

GPS-triggered stories, archival photos, and process videos revealed as you walk through the city.

3. Support options

Clear calls to action to visit studios, book workshops, buy directly, or donate to local initiatives.

Built for contributing a role in the preservation of traditional crafts

For Users

  • Use guided AR walks to understand the history behind each street and workshop
  • Follow curated routes that highlight living traditions still practiced today
  • Access resources, updates, and opportunities to get involved in preserving culture

For supporters

  • Discover how your small actions can help keep traditional skills alive
  • Connect with makers and learn the stories behind their craft
  • Explore ways to contribute — from sharing to visiting to buying directly
